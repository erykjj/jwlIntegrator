# Downgrading JW Library on iOS

This was contributed (not verified by me).

You will require an app called [*iMazing*](https://imazing.com/faq/imazing-trial) (which has a trial version).

The most important part: **get *JW Library* v15.6, sign it with your Apple ID, and save the `.IPA` file** so as to use it later (see the last slide: "Export .IPA").
